# Research run 2026-10-04 (the first one)

**Baseline:** `v0-773ea9`, the v3.1 rules on XRP, SOL and ETH.

On the history, by our own replay: 209 trades at +0.237R each, profit factor 1.50, max drawdown 11.1R. The forward test started today at 10:43 UTC and has no closed trades yet.

## What the pack showed

- **Longs earn, shorts barely do.** Longs: +0.416R a trade over 105 trades. Shorts: +0.056R over 104 trades. Probably partly the regime of June to October. There is no allowed rule for this; see "Ideas for later".
- **Trades with the 4h trend are weak.** With 4h only: +0.041R over 57 trades. With 1h and 4h: +0.066R over 33 trades. Trades against the 4h trend did much better: +0.36R to +0.41R.
  - The manual challenger `htf_against_4h` already tests this forward.
  - On the history it is not steady: +0.136R in the first half against the baseline's +0.253R.
- **The late hours are weaker.** 20:00–04:00 UTC: +0.116R and +0.178R a trade.
- **15m is better than 5m:** +0.257R against +0.178R. `tf_15m` and `trend15` test this forward.
- 34 of the 93 stopped trades had first reached +0.5R.
