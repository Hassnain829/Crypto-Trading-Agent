## Conclusions

- The baseline sits at a local optimum for its exit, entry and confirmation settings.
- Loosening filters adds trades without edge.
- More trades with the same quality need a new signal source with its own edge. Candidates:
  - 1h signals with Klinger: the AGENT-HTF charts do not have Klinger yet.
  - More coins, retested later with more data.
- The session filters (13–21 and 7–21 UTC) are the strongest forward challengers so far: +0.84R and +0.87R a trade, but only 17–22 trades. They need 100.
