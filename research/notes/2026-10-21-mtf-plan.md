# 2026-10-21: one decision per coin from the 5m and 15m charts, on 4 years (plan, written before the results)

## The user's questions

- Is strategy v3.1 (the baseline v0-773ea9) right?
- How can the agent stop opening long and short on the same coin?
- How can the 5m and 15m signals decide together and open a single trade?
- What should change in the strategy and the signal version?

Risk and budget stay as they are: A 3% / B 2%, 12% open-risk budget.

## Data

- **Main:** the 4-year set (data/research/long4), XRP, SOL and ETH, 2022-10-01 to 2026-10-18. These are the agent's engine trades on the Python copies of Q-Trend, Klinger and Squeeze.
- **Replication:** BTC (4 years), and the 10 check coins of the 1.5-year set.

## Candidates (fixed before the run)

| # | Candidate | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| C0 | now | every 5m and 15m signal on its own |
| C1 | 15m decides | a 5m signal only in the 15m Q-Trend direction; 15m signals always (the trend15 challenger) |
| C2 | C1 + one position per coin | while a coin has a position, every new signal of the coin is skipped |
| C3 | C1 + exit on the 15m flip | an open position is closed when the 15m Q-Trend flips against it (at the 1m open after the flip candle closes), so long and short are never open together |
| C4 | C3 + no duplicates | the user's design: the 15m sets the direction, the first 5m or 15m signal in that direction opens the one position, a flip closes it |
| C5 | 15m only | no 5m trades (the tf_15m challenger) |
| C6 | 15m only + one position per coin | |
| C7 | C1 + the 1h Q-Trend agrees | Q-Trend on 1h candles (made from the 15m candles), same inputs |
| C8 | C1 + the 4h Q-Trend agrees | the same on 4h; no value yet means allowed |
| C9 | C1 + skip B without a squeeze release | |

## Judgement

| Split | Period | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| In-sample | 2022-10-01 to 2024-10-01 | choosing |
| Out-of-sample | 2024-10-01 to 2026-10-18 | the decision, never used for choosing |

Measured per candidate:
- Trades, R a trade and total R in each year.
- Max drawdown in R, and double stops (a long and a short of a coin both stopped).
- $150 at today's rules: over all 4 years, and from $150 in each half.

A candidate is a recommendation only if:
- it beats C0 in-sample, and
- it beats C0 out-of-sample with a lower max drawdown, and
- it is not worse than C0 on BTC and on the check coins (R a trade).

### Verdict against the fixed rule

- **Only C5 (15m only) passes every check.** Out-of-sample it still loses (-18%).
- **C6 (15m only + one position per coin)** passes in-sample, out-of-sample and the check coins. It fails only BTC's R a trade (-0.080 vs -0.054), though it loses less money there.
  - It is the only candidate positive in every year on the main coins, including 2024-25.
  - It has the best R a trade and no double stops.
- **C9 (squeeze)** fails the in-sample return (+49% vs +77%). It has the lowest drawdown.
- **The user's design (C4, 5m entries with an exit on the 15m flip) fails.** The flip exit cuts winners, as exit_opposite_signal did on 2026-10-19.
- **The 1h and 4h Q-Trend filters fail.**

### What this means for the strategy

- 5m signals lose in every year, so the 5m chart should not open trades.
- "One position per coin" is the agent's existing `max_positions_per_coin: 1`. "15m only" is the baseline's `timeframes: ["15m"]` (the tf_15m challenger). C6 needs no new code: two settings.
