What the results mean:

- **Night hours:** like session_04_20, they are weak in only one half of the history. Hour filters look regime-dependent, so the forward session challengers decide.
- **Opposite-signal exit:** a Q-Trend flip on the trading chart is mostly noise inside the trade, and closing on it cuts winners.
- **Strong-sell shorts:** this passed the history screen. But the forward shorts were the better side (2026-10-18 notes), so the forward test may disagree.
