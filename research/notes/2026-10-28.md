## Key findings

- **`filters.macd_trend = true` hurts both halves**, unlike ZL/HTF filters that only hurt half 1. MACD side already agrees with most trades (like Supertrend direction = 95% agreement). The trades it removes are not bad ones. Do not propose further momentum-side filters.
- **Confirmation window floor confirmed at 3.** Window=2 still removes good late confirmations; the gap is now established at both ends (window=0 worse, window=2 worse). Late confirmations on candles 2-3 are among the good trades.
- **htf=1h+4h is too restrictive for 4 months of history** (13 trades in half 1). Any strict HTF combination needs at least 2 years of history to screen.
- **1.2x stop multiplier is worse** (+0.217R vs +0.264R). Confirmed from the 1h anchor lesson: wider stops reduce R per trade regardless of direction. The 1.0x swing is the ceiling too, not just the 0.8x floor.
- **entry.offset_r is incompatible with dip_5m_minutes.** The system rejects this combination. An additional entry offset on top of the 5m dip is architecturally blocked. Note this constraint.

## State of the allowed-changes space on this baseline

Most testable changes have now been explored:
- Filters: min_stop, session, trend, HTF (all 4 variants), ZL own, MACD trend — all worse
- Confirmation: window 0 and 2 worse; window 3 is the setting
- Entry: market entry worse; limit_minutes 5 worse; offset_r incompatible with dip
- Stop: lookback 8 and 15 worse; anchor 1h worse; multiplier 0.75, 0.8, 1.2 all worse; min_pct floors fail
- Exit: breakeven 1.0 and 1.25 worse; TP 1.25 and 2.0 worse; max_candles 48 worse; opposite signal worse

**Remaining genuinely untested:**
- `confirmation.window = 4 or 5` (wider; late confirmations already shown to help, so this might add more)
- `stop.multiplier = 0.85 or 0.9` (between tested 0.8 and 1.0; 0.8 was +0.038R; 0.9 might be +0.02-0.03R, still under bar)
- `exit.mode = "hybrid"` with partial_r between 1.0 and 1.5 (all shorter partial exits were worse; 1.25 partial might be novel)
- `trigger.long = "strong_buy"` alone (the combined strong/strong was unstable; only longs might be different, but few trades)
- `entry.limit_minutes = 2 or 3` (between tested 1 and 5; 1 is better than 5, so shorter is better; 1 is already the minimum)

None of these has a strong hypothesis. The v4 baseline has largely exhausted the allowed-changes list with no clear winner.
