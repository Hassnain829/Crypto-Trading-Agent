## Judgement

**The daily trend adds money in every window.**
- With it on the whole balance: 4 years +2,958% vs +1,215%, 2022-24 +270% vs +203%, 2024-26 +726% vs +335%, and since
  1 Aug +169% vs +156%.
- The drawdown rises a little: 63.7% vs 59.7% on 4 years, the same since 1 Aug.
- On its own it has the lowest drawdown (20%).
- The earlier research found it positive on every coin over 6 years.
- It adds open risk on top of the 15m trades: up to 6% more, so up to 18% in total.
- Today all four coins are well below their 20-day highs, so it would hold nothing yet.

**Engine 2 is positive over 4 years but not robust.**
- The 2022-24 half earned only +0.032R a trade, and with v4 that half ended lower than v4 alone (+174% vs +203%).
- It lost since 1 Aug (68 trades, -0.064R).
- It was about 0 on 9 other coins (engine2_check.py).
- At most a shadow test; it should not trade the demo.
