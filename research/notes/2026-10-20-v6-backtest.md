## Method

- **Studies:** the AGENT charts' version-6 studies (research/tools/agent_studies_v6.json), the same script versions and inputs, put on Market_check:
  - 5m: Q-Trend, Klinger, Squeeze, Zero Lag, ATP MACD
  - 15m: Q-Trend, Klinger, Squeeze, Zero Lag, Trendline
- **History:** 10,000 bars per chart, read with the agent's `read_history`.
- **Replay:** the whole agent on a copy of the journal (data/replay/journal-v6.db), by research/tools/v6_backtest.py:
  - SetupEngine with the baseline rules. Quality A comes from the 15m Trendline and the open interest (TradingView OI history, which matches Binance). The squeeze is recorded.
  - The Tracker, on Binance 1m candles.
  - The demo account with today's rules: $150, 2% risk, quality A 3%, 12% open-risk budget, overlapping positions handled.
- **Window:** trades from 2026-07-13 to 2026-10-18. The 15m history covers about 104 days; the 5m history covers about 35 days, so 5m trades start in mid-September.
