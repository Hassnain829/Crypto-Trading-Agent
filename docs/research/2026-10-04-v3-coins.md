**Data:**
- TradingView history of 10,000 bars per chart, read into `data/research/coins.db`. For the 5m charts that is about 35 days (from 2026-08-31); for 15m about 100 days (from 2026-06-25).

- 18 variants, each one change from v2, replayed on XRP, SOL, LINK, ADA and SUI in `data/research/v3.db`. The live journal was only read.

## Decision: v3

- **v3 = the v2 rules, traded on XRP and SOL only** (`paper_account.symbols`, `live_account.symbols`).
- The shadow book keeps testing LINK and every challenger, so the learning loop (Phase 5) can still promote a better variant, such as a session filter, from forward results.
- The forward test restarts on 2026-10-04 at $150.

## Caveats

- XRP and SOL were chosen after seeing the results, on about 100 days (15m) and 35 days (5m). This is selection on the same history. The forward test decides.
- Fewer coins means fewer trades: about 2.9 a day instead of about 4–6. At 100 trades, the go-live gate takes about 5 weeks.
- The coin results may depend on the market regime. LINK stays in the shadow book so a change can be seen.

## Round 2: a third coin and the user's ideas

The user wants at least 3 coins and live trading soon. Ideas tested, each as one change from v2:
- `hold15`: on 15m, hold until the opposite 15m signal.
- `trend15`: 5m signals only when the 15m Zero Lag trend agrees.
- `pull_0_3` / `pull_0_5`: limit orders 30–50% of the way toward the stop (5m pullback toward support), resting 30–60 minutes, with the target kept at the planned price.
- Also tested: break-even, partial profit at 1R, session, 4h/1h trend, own-chart Zero Lag and VWAP filters.

Coins: XRP, SOL, LINK, ADA, SUI, BTC, ETH, BNB, DOGE and AVAX, from 10,000-bar TradingView histories (data/research/v3b.db).

**Why LINK, ADA and SUI fail.** The stops are just as wide (about 2%). But only 35–37% of their trades reach the 1.5R target, against 45–48% for XRP and SOL. And 43–46% of their stopped trades had first gone +0.5R into profit: after a Q-Trend signal these coins chop instead of trending.
